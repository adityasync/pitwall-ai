# 01 — Product

## One line
A second screen that tells you **why** things happen in an F1 race, the moment you tap them.

## The fan problem
Watching F1 is easy. Understanding it is hard.
- A new fan sees a gap shrink or a driver pit and has no idea why it matters.
- The broadcast is fast, jargon-heavy and mostly in English.
- Devoted fans want real data (braking, pace, tyre age) but the tools are built for engineers and spread across many sites.
- India has a large and growing F1 audience (FanCode, part of Dream Sports, holds the India rights) and Hindi/Hinglish is how many of them talk about it.

## The solution
Tap a moment, understand why. Every explanation is written by an AI but built from numbers the code computed from real lap and telemetry data, with an evidence strip showing exactly which numbers were used.

## Three ways in (one dataset)
| Audience | Entry | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| Casual / new fan | Easy mode | One story at a time, big gaps, simple tyre health, every term explained in a line |
| Devoted fan | Pro workstation | Timing tower, track map with braking zones, traces, strategy, battles, radio |
| Analyst / student | Analysis suite | Session explorer, telemetry lab, qualifying, season view, export |

## Why this fits Dream11 / Dream Sports
- Fan-first: it increases understanding and engagement during a live sport, the same job fantasy and fan products do.
- Hinglish: built for the Indian audience.
- Trust: every number is labelled and the AI is grounded, which matters for any product people make decisions on.
- Extensible: the same "moment → explanation" engine works for cricket (ball-by-ball data) later. Mention this in the demo; do not build it.

## Scope
**In scope:** replay of historical races (2023 onwards), Easy and Pro modes, AI explanations and Ask, recap, compare, strategy, pace, battles, driver pages, explorer, telemetry lab, methods page, landing page.

**Out of scope:** real-time live data (paid), betting or fantasy features, accounts and servers with databases, video, mobile apps.

## Success criteria for the submission
1. A reviewer opens the link and within 30 seconds taps a moment and gets a clear, grounded explanation.
2. Easy mode is understandable by someone who has never watched F1.
3. Pro mode shows real analysis (traces, braking zones, strategy) with honest labels.
4. Hinglish toggle works everywhere an AI sentence appears.
5. Works on a phone.
6. The 200-word note and a 2-minute demo script are ready.

## The hero flow (must be flawless)
Landing → Pick a race → Easy or Pro → Tap a moment → "Why?" explanation with evidence → Open the analysis page or Ask the AI.

## Principles
Honest over impressive. Few screens done well beat many screens done thinly. Say "the data cannot show this" instead of guessing.
